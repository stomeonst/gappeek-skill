# Frozen evidence example: US women's running shoes

This dated example shows how GapPeek keeps demand, supply, competition, price,
and evidence boundaries in one report. It does not represent a live market
reading or a recommendation to buy, source, or list a product.

## Scope

| Field | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Market | US women's running shoes |
| Marketplace | Temu |
| Observation date | August 27, 2026 |
| Conclusion grade | Observation |

## Evidence

| Metric | Recorded value |
| --- | ---: |
| Monthly sales | 10,513 |
| Monthly sales change | +28.55% |
| Monitored products | 500 |
| Monitored sellers | 251 |
| Leading product concentration | 90.6% |
| Leading seller concentration | 100% |
| Median observed price | $13.12 |

## Reading the result

### 1. Demand

The recorded monthly sales change is positive. The value belongs to the dated
observation and cannot be treated as a live reading.

### 2. Supply

The observation contains 500 products and 251 sellers. These are monitored
counts for the stated analysis scope, not claims about every product or seller
on the marketplace.

### 3. Competition

The concentration values show that the leading portion of the observed market
holds a large share. Demand growth and concentrated competition therefore need
to be considered together.

### 4. Price

The median observed price is $13.12. This is a market observation, not a
recommended selling price or a profit calculation.

## Example agent summary

```text
Requested direction: US women's running shoes

Monthly sales change: +28.55%
Monitored products: 500
Monitored sellers: 251
Median observed price: $13.12

Conclusion grade: Observation
Demand growth is visible, while the observed competition structure is highly
concentrated. The evidence does not support a sales or profit guarantee.
```

## What this example demonstrates

1. Every claim stays tied to a named market and observation date.
2. Product, seller, demand, price, and concentration evidence appear together.
3. Monitored counts are not described as complete marketplace totals.
4. A positive demand signal does not automatically become a product
   recommendation.
5. The report keeps a visible conclusion grade.
